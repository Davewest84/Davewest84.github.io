Internationally, today is a landmark day for global energy markets. The UAE officially leaves OPEC, effective today, after nearly 60 years in the cartel. The move comes after weeks of Iranian missile and drone attacks on UAE shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, which has severely constrained Gulf oil exports. Brent crude settled around 118 dollars a barrel yesterday, up more than six per cent. The UAE wants to push towards five million barrels a day of production capacity, free from OPEC output limits, but that ambition depends entirely on reopening the Strait. The broader Iran standoff shows no sign of easing, with Trump signalling the naval blockade will continue until a nuclear deal is reached.
