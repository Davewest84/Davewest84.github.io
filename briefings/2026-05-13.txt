Now, the King's Speech has a major NHS angle. The government is expected to introduce a Health Plan Bill that will formally abolish NHS England, with the aim of completing that by April 2027. The bill will also restructure integrated care boards, changing their membership and functions. Healthwatch England and more than 150 local Healthwatches will be abolished. The Health Services Safety Investigations Body will be folded into the Care Quality Commission, and the Patient Safety Commissioner's functions will transfer to the MHRA. There is also a mandate for a single digital patient record across all trusts. This is structural reorganisation on a scale we have not seen since 2012, and it will directly affect the working lives of virtually every senior NHS leader.
