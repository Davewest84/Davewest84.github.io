Elsewhere in the UK, a significant milestone for renters passes today. The Renters' Rights Act abolished Section 21 no-fault evictions when it came into force on the first of May, and today, the 31st, is the deadline for all landlords in England to have provided tenants with a copy of the official Renters' Rights Act information sheet. Fines of up to seven thousand pounds per tenancy apply for non-compliance. It marks the practical conclusion of one of the biggest changes to the private rental market in a generation. Turning to international news. The Romania drone strike from Friday continues to dominate European security discussions. A Russian drone crashed into a residential apartment building in the eastern Romanian city of Galati, injuring two people. It is the 28th time Russian drones have breached Romanian airspace since Moscow began attacking Ukrainian ports along the Danube, but the first to hit a populated area and cause casualties in a NATO member state. Romania has expelled a Russian diplomat and closed the Russian consulate in Constanta.
