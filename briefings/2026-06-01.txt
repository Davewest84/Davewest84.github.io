On health, the government has published a significant announcement today. DHSC says the NHS Modernisation Bill will introduce a single patient record, claiming it will result in 20,000 fewer A&E visits a year by allowing clinicians across England to see a patient's full medical history regardless of where they are treated. All NHS providers, including hospitals and GPs, will be required to share data so that the right clinicians can securely access a patient's full record. Improved access is expected from 2027, starting with maternity and frailty care, before a wider rollout via the NHS App. The bill also formally abolishes NHS England, restructures integrated care board commissioning duties, and abolishes Healthwatch in favour of a new DHSC patient voice function.

The Golani Brigade raised the Israeli flag over the castle on Saturday, and Defence Minister Israel Katz said troops will remain as part of a permanent security zone, going well beyond the original ceasefire terms.
