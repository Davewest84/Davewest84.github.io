On health, the BMA's ballot on the government's pay offer to resident doctors opens at 3pm today and runs until noon on Friday the 26th of June. The walkout that had been scheduled for the 15th to the 19th was called off at the last minute after the government tabled a deal worth an average 6.6 per cent uplift, to be delivered by April 2027. Pay increases range from 3.5 to 7.1 per cent depending on the pay point, and the offer also includes moving locally employed doctors onto substantive contracts from August.
