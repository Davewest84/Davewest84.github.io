Segments run 1 (narrowest range of challenges) to 4 (broadest). Deficit override: a trust in financial deficit cannot be placed above segment 3 however well it scores on delivery; "applied" means the trust would otherwise have been in segment 1 or 2. Q4 restated applies the 2026/27 score thresholds and the override to Q4 2025/26 scores. In 2025/26 segments were fixed quartiles, so segment 4 always held a quarter of trusts; from 2026/27 they are fixed score thresholds with no cap on segment size.

Source: HSJ analysis of NHS England NHS Oversight Framework segmentation and league tables, Q4 2025/26 and Q1 2026/27. Ranks are within trust group. Q1 2026/27 covers 202 trusts, down from 205 after mergers; three trusts in the Q4 table are not in the Q1 table and are excluded. The scored metric set also changed for 2026/27 (46 metrics, of which 21 are new), so score movements are not a pure measure of performance change.
