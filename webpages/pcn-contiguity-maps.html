PCN Non-Contiguity: The Neighbourhood Health Problem

Each dot is a GP practice, coloured by its PCN membership. If PCNs served contiguous areas, you would see solid blocks of colour. Where colours are intermingled, PCN territories are fragmented — the practices of one PCN are separated by practices belonging to other PCNs.

Methodology: Delaunay triangulation of all 6,106 GP practices identifies natural geographic neighbours. Practices in the same building or within 500m are treated as adjacent. A PCN is “non-contiguous” if its members do not form a connected territory. Each panel below shows the practices whose own ICB matches the labelled area (or, for the Northumberland panel, a specific named PCN); the map view is auto-fitted to those practices.
