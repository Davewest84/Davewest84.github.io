MSOA-Disconnected PCNs — the locality test

A PCN is “MSOA-disconnected” if its practices lie in MSOA neighbourhoods that don’t form a single connected blob (where two MSOAs are adjacent if any pair of their GP practices is geographically next to each other). Nationally, 258 of 1,210 multi-practice PCNs (21.3%) are MSOA-disconnected — their members live in genuinely separate localities, not just on the wrong side of a Voronoi boundary.

MSOA-disconnected — thick red ring on yellow fill. The PCN’s practices live in two or more disjoint neighbourhood blobs.
Non-contiguous (locality OK) — thin orange ring. Flagged by the original Delaunay test, but the practices’ MSOAs still touch each other — same locality, just on the wrong side of a Voronoi boundary.
Coherent — thin grey ring. Members form one connected MSOA blob.
