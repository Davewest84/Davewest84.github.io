NHS England capability ratings, Q3 2025/26 — chair and CEO in post during the assessment window (Aug–Dec 2025) with start dates, and changes since

Ratings published by NHSE on 14 April 2026. Table compiled 16 April 2026. Sources: NHSE methodology PDF; trust websites, annual reports and press releases; HSJ search and Content APIs. Italic red text indicates the start date could not be confirmed from public sources.

What this is. NHS England's NHS Oversight Framework now combines a capability rating with delivery against national expectations to drive its regulatory approach. The inaugural process assessed all 204 trusts between August and December 2025, moderated by regional and national directors and ratified by the NHSE executive. Four levels: Green, Amber/Green, Amber/Red, Red.

Red is defined as: "Significant concerns arising from poor delivery, governance and other issues" — material or long-running concerns management has been unable to grip; trust in breach of licence or likely to be. Fourteen trusts (6.9%) are red-rated.

Methodology note. Capability ratings derived from trusts' own self-assessment (Aug–Oct 2025) against the six domains of The Insightful Provider Board — strategy and planning, quality of care, people and culture, access, productivity, finance — reviewed by NHSE regional teams alongside third-party intelligence. Two-stage moderation by regional directors and national/regional directors before executive ratification. Ratings reflect a view relating to Q3 2025/26 and will be refreshed quarterly from Q4 2025/26.

Date source caveats. Start dates drawn from trust press releases, board pages and annual reports where available, with HSJ cross-checks. Four items were not cleanly confirmed in public sources and are flagged inline: Martin Hodgson's substantive start at East Lancashire; Dawn Scrafield's substantive start at MSE; Adam Sewell-Jones's MSE start date; the precise substantive start for Jane Tomkinson at Countess of Chester.
