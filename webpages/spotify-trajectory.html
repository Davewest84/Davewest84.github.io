Bob Dylan is the spine. #1 in eight separate years and never out of the top 10. The 2020 lockdown spike (21h that year alone) is when Self Portrait got its own deep dive.

The "obsession of the year" is real. Bill Callahan in 2021. Waxahatchee in 2024 (27 hours — the biggest single-year fixation in the dataset). Cameron Winter in 2025.

Drift toward freak folk. The last two years pull in Lankum, Geese, Jake Xerxes Fussell, Adrianne Lenker, Cameron Winter — more experimental, more sparse, less polished alt-country.
