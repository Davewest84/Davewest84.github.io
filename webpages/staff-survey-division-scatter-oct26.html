Read with care. Divisions are defined by each trust, so like is not always compared with like: some are whole departments, others a small team or a mix of services. Small slices of a trust (left of the chart) swing more widely, so compare a division only with its own trust or with others of similar size, not as a ranking.

Source: NHS Staff Survey 2025 breakdown reports, HSJ analysis. Acute trusts; divisions with 50 or more responses. Service types grouped by HSJ.
